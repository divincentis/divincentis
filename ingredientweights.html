Notes

  • Weights are approximate and assume ingredients are measured by the standard scoop-and-level method (not sifted unless noted).
  • For best baking results, weigh ingredients rather than measuring by volume — especially flours, which can vary by 20% or more depending on how they're scooped and settled.
  • 1 US cup = 236.6 ml; 1 US tablespoon = 14.8 ml; 1 US teaspoon = 4.9 ml.
