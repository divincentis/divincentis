Notes

  • The batter should be quite thin - thinner than normal pancake batter. Add milk gradually until you reach the right consistency.
  • These pancakes are meant to be thin, more like crepes.
  • The first pancake often doesn't turn out perfect - this is normal and helps season the pan!
  • You can keep the cooked pancakes warm in a low oven (200°F) while making the rest.
  • These are best enjoyed fresh and warm, rolled up with butter and sugar as per family tradition.
