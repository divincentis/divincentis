Notes

  • This is a Chinese dish, the name translates to: 拍 (Pāi) = smack/pat, 黄瓜 (Huáng Guā) = cucumber.
  • English cucumbers are preferred as they have fewer seeds and thinner skin than regular cucumbers.
  • Don't skip the salting step - it helps remove excess water and creates a crunchier texture.
  • Adjust the amount of red pepper flakes based on your preferred level of heat.
