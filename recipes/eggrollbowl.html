Notes

  • Two 14 oz bags of cole slaw mix is a bit more than the original 24 oz, which makes it a little more veggie-heavy. It cooks down a lot.
  • To roast the tofu: press it, cut into ½-inch cubes, toss with a little oil, and roast at 425°F (220°C) for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden. Do this before you start cooking, or use store-bought baked tofu.
  • Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Add the wonton chips just before serving so they stay crunchy.
